## Modules

| Module | Syllabus | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Module 1 | Introduction – brain and computer; biological and artificial neuron models – McCulloch-Pitts (MP), Adaline, Perceptron. Characteristics and applications of ANN. Classification of ANN – connectivity, neural dynamics, learning strategy, learning rules. Learning in simple neurons – the perceptron, the perceptron learning rule, limitations of the perceptron, the multilayer perceptron, the backpropagation network. | Pending |
| Module 2 | Adaptive Resonance Theory (ART) – architecture and operation, the ART algorithm, training the ART network, classification. Associative memory, Bi-directional Associative Memory (BAM). Applications of neural nets – pattern recognition. Kohonen self-organizing networks – introduction, the Kohonen algorithm, weight training, neighbourhoods, reducing the neighbourhood. | Pending |
| Module 3 | Fuzzy systems – uncertainty and information, fuzzy sets and membership, fuzziness, classical vs fuzzy sets – operations and properties, properties of membership functions, fuzzification, defuzzification to crisp sets, fuzzy logic, applications. Genetic algorithms – fundamentals, creating offspring, reproduction, genetic modelling, operators, applications. | Pending |
