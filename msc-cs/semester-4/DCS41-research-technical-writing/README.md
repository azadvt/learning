## Modules

| Module | Syllabus | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Module 1 | Research – objectives and types of research; motivation and objectives; defining and formulating the research problem; importance of literature review; identifying gap areas from the literature review. Definitions and types of research. The scientific method – observation, questions, hypothesis, experimentation, critical communication. Presenting and publishing research work – seminar, workshop, symposium, conference. | Pending |
| Module 2 | Reporting and thesis writing – writing a proposal, structure and components of scientific reports. Types of report – technical reports and thesis. Steps in preparation, layout, illustrations and tables, bibliography, referencing and footnotes. Technical writing in LaTeX – compilation, formatting, books as chapters, header and footer, chapters and sections, lists, tables, images, labels and references, index, list of figures and tables, math formulae, hyperlinks, bookmarks, bibliography, troubleshooting. Classes – article, book, report, slides, IEEE Tran; writing research papers. | Pending |
| Module 3 | Python – environment variables. Collections – lists, tuples, sets, dictionaries, sorting dictionaries, copying collections. String operations, the format method. Functions – user-defined, optional parameters, passing collections, variable arguments, scope, mapping functions in a dictionary, lambda, closures. Iterators, text and binary handling – iteration protocol, iterable objects, generators and generator expressions, data processing pipelines. Classes, custom exception classes, pydoc, handling multiple exceptions, Python & CGI, Python with databases. | Pending |
