# LGBTQ Terminology

## The Acronym

- **L** — Lesbian: A woman attracted to other women
- **G** — Gay: A person attracted to the same gender (often used for men)
- **B** — Bisexual: A person attracted to more than one gender
- **T** — Transgender: A person whose gender identity differs from their sex assigned at birth
- **Q** — Queer / Questioning: An umbrella term for non-heterosexual/non-cisgender identities, or someone exploring their identity

## Extended Versions

- **LGBTQIA+**
  - **I** — Intersex: Born with sex characteristics that don't fit typical male/female definitions
  - **A** — Asexual / Aromantic / Agender
  - **+** — Includes all other identities not specifically covered

## Key Terms

- **Cisgender** — A person whose gender identity matches their sex assigned at birth
- **Non-binary** — A person who doesn't identify exclusively as male or female
- **Pansexual** — Attraction regardless of gender
- **Gender expression** — How a person outwardly shows their gender (clothing, behavior, appearance)
- **Gender identity** — A person's internal sense of their own gender
- **Sexual orientation** — Who a person is attracted to
- **Transition** — The process of aligning one's life with their gender identity (social, medical, or legal)
- **Ally** — A person who supports LGBTQ rights but doesn't identify as LGBTQ
- **Coming out** — The process of revealing one's sexual orientation or gender identity
