# LGBTQ Rights & Laws

## Global Overview

- **Same-sex marriage** is legal in 35+ countries (as of 2024)
- **Criminalization**: Same-sex relations are still illegal in ~60+ countries, with some imposing the death penalty

## Key Legal Concepts

- **Anti-discrimination laws** — Protect LGBTQ people in employment, housing, and public services
- **Hate crime laws** — Enhanced penalties for crimes motivated by bias against sexual orientation or gender identity
- **Gender recognition laws** — Allow transgender people to legally change their gender marker
- **Conversion therapy bans** — Laws that prohibit practices attempting to change someone's sexual orientation or gender identity
