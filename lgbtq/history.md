# LGBTQ History

## Key Events

- **1969 — Stonewall Riots** (New York City): A series of protests by the LGBTQ community against police raids. Widely considered the catalyst for the modern LGBTQ rights movement.

- **1973** — The American Psychiatric Association removes homosexuality from its list of mental disorders.

- **1978 — Rainbow Flag**: Gilbert Baker designs the first rainbow pride flag in San Francisco.

- **1981–1990s — AIDS Crisis**: The HIV/AIDS epidemic disproportionately affected gay men. Government inaction led to activist groups like ACT UP demanding research and treatment.

- **2001 — Netherlands**: First country to legalize same-sex marriage.

- **2015 — Obergefell v. Hodges**: U.S. Supreme Court legalizes same-sex marriage nationwide.

- **2020** — U.S. Supreme Court rules in *Bostock v. Clayton County* that Title VII protects LGBTQ employees from workplace discrimination.

## Notable Figures

- **Marsha P. Johnson** — Transgender activist, key figure at Stonewall
- **Harvey Milk** — First openly gay elected official in California (1977)
- **Bayard Rustin** — Civil rights leader, organized the 1963 March on Washington
- **Alan Turing** — Mathematician and codebreaker, persecuted for being gay
