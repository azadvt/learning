# LGBTQ Learning Resources

A structured path from beginner to deep understanding.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Start Here)

### Books
- **"Gender Queer" by Maia Kobabe** — A graphic memoir, very accessible starting point
- **"This Book is Gay" by Juno Dawson** — Covers basics of sexuality and gender identity clearly
- **"LGBTQ+ History" by Meg-John Barker** — Visual, beginner-friendly overview

### Watch
- **"The Death and Life of Marsha P. Johnson"** (Netflix) — Stonewall and trans activism
- **"Disclosure"** (Netflix) — How media shaped perception of transgender people
- **"Pride"** (2014 film) — True story of LGBTQ activists supporting miners in 1980s UK

## Phase 2: History & Context

### Books
- **"Stonewall" by Martin Duberman** — The definitive account of the 1969 uprising
- **"And the Band Played On" by Randy Shilts** — The AIDS crisis, essential reading
- **"A Queer History of the United States" by Michael Bronski** — 400 years of LGBTQ American history

### Watch
- **"How to Survive a Plague"** (documentary) — AIDS activism and ACT UP
- **"Paris is Burning"** (1990) — Ball culture, drag, and chosen family

## Phase 3: Gender, Identity & Theory

### Books
- **"Gender Trouble" by Judith Butler** — Academic but foundational text on gender theory
- **"Whipping Girl" by Julia Serano** — Transgender experience and feminism
- **"Redefining Realness" by Janet Mock** — Memoir on being a trans woman of color

## Phase 4: Global & Intersectional Perspectives

### Books
- **"No Ashes in the Fire" by Darnell L. Moore** — Race, sexuality, and identity
- **"Boy Erased" by Garrard Conley** — Conversion therapy memoir
- **"Queer: A Graphic History" by Meg-John Barker** — Queer theory made visual

### Research Organizations
- **ILGA World** (ilga.org) — Global data on LGBTQ laws and rights
- **Human Rights Campaign** (hrc.org) — U.S.-focused resources
- **Stonewall UK** (stonewall.org.uk) — UK-focused resources
